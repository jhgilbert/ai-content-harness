**Let admins export the member list as CSV**

As a workspace admin, I want to export every member with their role, so that I can audit access in a spreadsheet.

### Acceptance criteria

- Given I am an admin, when I click Export, then a CSV downloads.
- Given I am not an admin, when I open the members page, then the Export button is hidden.
