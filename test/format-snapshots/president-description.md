# George Washington

## Overview

George Washington was the 1st president of the United States. He led the Continental Army to victory in the American Revolutionary War, presided over the Constitutional Convention, and set many of the precedents that shaped the presidency.

He was born in 1732, and he died in 1799.

## Terms

- April 30, 1789 to March 4, 1793, with John Adams as vice president
- March 4, 1793 to March 4, 1797, with John Adams as vice president
